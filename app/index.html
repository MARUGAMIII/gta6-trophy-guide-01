Roadmap to the platinum Vice Got a Price! · about 200 hours · difficulty 4/10 · one playthrough

No online trophies. No difficulty trophies, no mission medals, no New Game+. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter (15 manual slots + 1 autosave). Cheats switch off trophies and saving — keep them off on your trophy save.

  1. Step 1 — Finish the story 60–80 hours

    Play the 5 chapters and enjoy it. The 100% checklist is not viewable until after the story, but progress made during it already counts. Do optional missions as they appear (you need any 100 for 100%), scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app (200 of 277 base models needed) and study animals through the binoculars (100 of 255). Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 — it is the one trophy that can be missed (see Positive Mental Attitude).

  2. Step 2 — 100% completion checklist & challenges about 100 hours

    After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. The list is lenient — closer to 60% true completion. The only tasks that need everything are robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests. You need any 70 of the 80 challenges, 300 stunts, and 15 of 18 meditations. Everything is still doable in free-roam.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and clean-up about 10 hours

    Finish the 8 stranger strands that count (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete and more), the side-character finales, and any trophies left over. Most of them pop while you work through Step 2. When the last one pops, the platinum follows.

⚠ Only one trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — Lucia’s Chapter 1 balcony meditation disappears after Chapter 1. Everything else stays available after the story.
